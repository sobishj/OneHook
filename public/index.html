Install SprintGames

Add to your Home Screen for instant play & offline access

Tap the button below to add SprintGames directly to your phone's home screen or apps list:

📱 iPhone & iPad (Safari)
  1. 1
    Tap the Share button ⎋ (Square with arrow up) at the bottom or top of Safari
  2. 2
    Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" ➕ icon in the action list
  3. 3
    Tap "Add" in the top-right corner SprintGames icon will appear on your home screen!
🤖 Android (Chrome / Samsung / Firefox)
  1. 1
    Tap the browser Menu ⋮ (Three dots at top-right) or ≡ in Samsung Internet
  2. 2
    Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen" Follow the quick on-screen prompt to install
💻 Desktop Browser

Click the Install ⊕ button in your browser's address bar or click below.
